About & Expo Info

Project overview and technical architecture

LifeLine – Blood Bank Management System

College: Madhav Institute of Technology & Science

LifeLine helps a blood bank register donors, monitor stock of all eight blood groups, raise low-stock alerts, issue units during emergencies and check donor–recipient compatibility.

  • Donor registration with validation (unique ID, 10-digit phone)
  • Live inventory with low-stock alerts (3 units or fewer)
  • Emergency issue of units with automatic stock deduction
  • Search by blood group with compatibility chart
  • Secure staff login with logout
  • Printable donation certificate for every donor

Frontend

HTML5 and CSS3 responsive dashboard UI.

Logic

Vanilla JavaScript for validation, search, compatibility and stock handling.

Storage

Browser localStorage keeps data saved after refresh.
